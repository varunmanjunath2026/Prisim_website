Product

WhatsApp Bot Automation Dashboard

Customers message at all hours, and a lead who waits too long books somewhere else. This product answers them in seconds and keeps your whole team working from one place.

The problemTen tools that don't talk to each other

Most businesses run on WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, a mail inbox, a calendar, sticky notes and a separate billing tool. Messages get missed, slots get double-booked and follow-ups are forgotten.

The WhatsApp Bot Automation Dashboard brings all of that into one system, with an AI agent doing the first reply.

FeaturesWhat it does

  • AI agent that replies, books and sellsTrained on your own documents. It answers questions on price and timings, books slots and hands the chat to your team when a person is needed.
  • Every channel, one inboxWhatsApp, Instagram, Google, email and your website messages in one shared inbox, with chats assigned to team members.
  • Booking engineCustomers pick a slot and pay by UPI without leaving WhatsApp, and your calendar updates itself.
  • Leads added automaticallyEvery new chat becomes a lead in your CRM, with its source and stage.
  • Campaigns and follow-upsSend personal follow-up messages to the right customers, and see who replied.
  • WorkflowsAutomate the steps after every order or booking, such as sending a WhatsApp message or creating an invoice.
  • TicketingEvery customer request gets an owner and a deadline, so nothing is left open.
  • Business insightsLive numbers for sales, bookings and your team, plus alerts on what needs fixing, such as slow replies.
  • WebfrontsPages for booking, ordering and paying that open inside WhatsApp.
  • Team rolesEveryone sees only what their role allows.

Who it's forBuilt for businesses that live on WhatsApp

  • Clinics & hospitals
  • Hotels & restaurants
  • Real estate
  • Education
  • Distribution & trading

Product

Accounting & Billing Software

Made for hotels, restaurants and any food business with more than one outlet. Staff enter what happened, and the software does the accounting.

How it worksFrom a pile of bills to one P&L, in three steps

  • 1. CaptureUpload the day book, scan a bill with your phone camera or add a petty-cash expense. Handwritten bills are read by AI, and Kannada item names are translated.
  • 2. CheckEvery day must match its closing balance, every bill must add up, and the same bill can't be posted twice. A vendor spelt two ways still lands in one ledger.
  • 3. ReportVouchers are written for you, and every report reads from them: P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, food cost, purchases, vendors and expenses.
  • Every figure traceableClick any number in a report to see the exact bill, day or journal entry it came from.

FeaturesWhat owners get

  • Every outlet on one dashboardDaily sales, cash and food cost for each outlet and the central kitchen, side by side.
  • Answers every morningFood cost this month, money owed to vendors, the best-selling outlet and online-order sales, at a glance.
  • Alerts before problems growA missing day book, a bill waiting to be posted, food cost over target, a sudden price rise or kitchen stock running low.
  • Online orders split outSwiggy and Zomato sales tracked separately from counter sales, every day.
  • Central kitchen stockIndents in, goods out and stock on hand, with reorder alerts.
  • Branch-limited loginsEach outlet sees only its own records, and head office sees everything.
  • Role permissions and audit logChoose what each role can view, create, edit, approve or delete. Every save and upload is logged with who and when.
  • Works anywhereOn phone, tablet and desktop, saving to the cloud as you go.
