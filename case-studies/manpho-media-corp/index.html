One Connected Platform for Four Business Verticals

Manpho Media Corp

Custom Software Development + Digital Marketing

Unified four business verticals with one lead-generation system.

4Business Verticals Connected
7 WeeksPlatform Development

The goalOne system for a business with many sides

Manpho Media Corp is a Bengaluru group that runs several businesses under one roof, including events, hotels and gifting. Each one had its own enquiries, its own customers and its own way of working.

The group needed every lead to land in one place, and a single platform the teams could run their day-to-day work on, instead of separate tools that did not talk to each other.

What we didOne connected platform, built in seven weeks

Prisim designed and built a custom platform that links Manpho's public website, its sales team and its operations teams. An enquiry made on the website becomes a lead in the sales dashboard straight away, and can move all the way to a quote and a confirmed booking without being typed in twice.

Alongside the software, Prisim ran the digital marketing that feeds new enquiries into the system.

Inside the platformWhat each team gets

  • Website to bookingWebsite enquiry, lead, follow-ups, a quotation PDF and conversion into a confirmed booking, in one flow.
  • Event operationsTasks, checklists, vendors and purchase orders, inventory, documents and support tickets for every event.
  • Hotel managementRoom grid, walk-ins, check-in and check-out, housekeeping with photo proof, and maintenance requests.
  • Client portalClients follow progress, approve items, request changes, pay, share guest lists and message the team.

The resultFour business verticals running on one system

Manpho's four business verticals now share one lead-generation system and one platform. The sales team sees every enquiry in one dashboard, and each team only sees the screens its role needs.
