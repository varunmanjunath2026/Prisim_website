Turning Local Brand Visibility Into Real Customer Visits

Chandana Car Care

The goalGet local drivers to choose Chandana

Chandana Car Care is a local car care business. Customers come from the surrounding area, so what matters is being seen by drivers nearby and earning their trust before their first visit.

What we didTargeted local ads and a face behind the brand

Prisim ran Meta Ads campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, aimed at people in Chandana's local area, so the budget went to drivers who could actually visit.

To build trust, Prisim created an owner-reveal video campaign that introduced the person behind the business. People are far more comfortable handing over their car when they know who is looking after it.

The campaignHow the pieces fit together

  • Targeted local campaignsMeta Ads aimed at nearby drivers, not a broad audience who could never visit.
  • Owner-reveal videoA video campaign putting the owner in front of customers to build trust.
  • From views to visitsLocal visibility and trust work together to turn attention into customer visits.
