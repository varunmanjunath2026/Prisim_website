Connecting Intelligent Call Analytics With Digital Growth

Relicomp Elevators

AI Software Development + Digital Marketing

Connected automated call analytics with a stronger digital presence.

AIAutomated Call Analytics
360°Digital Growth Infrastructure

The goalMake every customer call count

Relicomp Elevators is an elevator company. Much of its new business starts with a phone call, so knowing what happens on those calls matters as much as getting the phone to ring.

Relicomp wanted a clearer picture of its calls and a stronger online presence to bring more of them in.

What we didAI call analytics, connected to digital growth

Prisim built AI software that analyses Relicomp's calls automatically, so the team gets insight from every conversation without listening back to recordings by hand.

Alongside it, Prisim strengthened Relicomp's digital presence with marketing that brings in new enquiries, so the calls coming in and the insight coming out are part of one system.

What changedFrom scattered calls to a connected growth system

  • Automated call analyticsCalls are analysed by AI automatically, with no manual review needed.
  • 360° digital presenceA stronger online presence that brings in new enquiries.
  • One connected loopMarketing brings in the calls, and call analytics shows what is working.
