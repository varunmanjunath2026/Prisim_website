Scaling Wedding Experiences Through Marketing & Technology

Tyaara Weddings

Digital Marketing + Custom Software Development

Built a predictable funnel and streamlined wedding operations with WedOps.

2xLead Volume in 35 Days
3xStandard Pricing Growth

The goalMore wedding enquiries, and less chaos once a wedding is booked

Tyaara Weddings is a wedding planning business. It wanted a steady, predictable flow of new enquiries instead of waiting on word of mouth, and a simpler way to keep each booked wedding on track.

Running a wedding means juggling many vendors, advance payments and balances against a fixed date. When that lives in notebooks and chat threads, payments get missed and planners spend their time chasing numbers instead of couples.

What we didA marketing funnel to bring couples in, and software to run the weddings

On the marketing side, Prisim built a lead funnel that turns people looking for a wedding planner into enquiries for Tyaara. Within 35 days, lead volume doubled.

On the operations side, Prisim built WedOps, custom wedding-operations software that keeps every wedding, vendor and payment in one place.

Inside WedOpsThe parts the team uses every week

  • Wedding countdownEach wedding shows how many days are left, so the team always knows what is coming up next.
  • Money at a glanceTotals for every wedding, plus the payments due or overdue in the next 14 days.
  • Vendor trackingEvery vendor with the amount quoted, the advance paid and the balance still owed, which updates automatically.
  • All weddings in one listAny number of weddings, each with its vendor count and outstanding balance, and one click to switch between them.

The resultA busier pipeline and a business that could charge more

Lead volume doubled in 35 days. With a steady flow of enquiries and smoother delivery, Tyaara grew its standard pricing three times over.

The team now tracks each wedding, its vendors and its payments from one screen.
